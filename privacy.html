Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Lyricpad is a lyric-writing tool that runs entirely in your web browser. It has no server and no database of its own. It collects only anonymous visit statistics (below), and it does not sell or share information about you. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Your lyrics and settings

The song you are working on, and your settings (such as theme, rhyme options and AI provider choice), are stored in your own browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to the developers of Lyricpad. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose to save songs to Google Drive, Lyricpad asks Google for permission to use your Drive with the drive.file scope. This lets Lyricpad create a “Lyricpad” folder and see, create and edit only the files it creates there. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

  • Songs are saved as text files in your own Google Drive, under your Google account. The developers of Lyricpad cannot see them.
  • The sign-in token Google gives Lyricpad (valid for one hour) and your Google account's email address are kept only in your browser. The token is used only to read and write those song files; the email address is used only to tell Google which account to renew access for. Neither is ever sent anywhere except to Google. Disconnecting (below) removes both from your browser.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the Songs window in Lyricpad, or remove Lyricpad's access in your Google Account settings. Your saved songs stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Lyricpad's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained from Google is used only to provide the save-and-open feature you asked for; it is not transferred to anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

AI line suggestions (optional)

If you use “Suggest lines”, you provide your own API key for Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini). When you ask for suggestions, Lyricpad sends your song's title, lyrics, topic and options directly from your browser to the provider you picked, and nowhere else. That provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to that request (Anthropic, Google). Your API key is stored only in your browser.

Visit statistics

Lyricpad's pages load Cloudflare Web Analytics so we can see how many people use it. It reports, in aggregate: which page was visited and the site you came from; your browser, operating system and device type; your country, worked out from your IP address; and how quickly the page loaded. Cloudflare states that it uses no cookies or other browser storage, does not fingerprint visitors, and does not use your IP address to identify or track you. It never sees your lyrics, songs, Google Drive data or API keys. Content blockers usually block it, and Lyricpad works the same either way. Cloudflare's privacy policy applies. The same statistics run on all the GP Song Tools; see the suite privacy page.

Hosting

Lyricpad's pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads the site; see GitHub's privacy statement. Apart from the visit statistics above, Lyricpad uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Children

Lyricpad is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
